The Core Method

Why standard math practice doesn't fix gaps

  1. Pick the topic that feels hard.You answer 2 to 3 short diagnostic checks. A third question appears only if the first two disagree, preventing careless slips from deciding the outcome.
  2. If you slip, Gap Finder checks the earlier foundation.Instead of simply drilling you on the same hard problem, the app steps backward along prerequisite branches to test the underlying rules.
  3. It stops at the root cause.A failed concept whose own prerequisites are all secure is marked as the root cause. Everything built on top of it unlocks once this gap is repaired.
  4. You get a focused lesson and a 1-week plan.A targeted mini-lesson explains the exact misconception, followed by an actionable spaced repetition schedule with calendar dates.
