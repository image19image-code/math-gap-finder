Method

How it works

  1. Pick the topic that feels hard.You answer 2 to 3 short questions on it. A third question only appears if the first two disagree.
  2. If you slip, the app checks prerequisites.It walks backward through the topics that came before, one at a time, asking the same 2–3 checks.
  3. It finds the root gap.It stops at the earliest idea you missed — the real cause — and marks everything above it that depends on that idea.
  4. You get a mini-lesson and a plan.A short lesson for the root concept, your exact mistakes explained, and a 1-week review schedule with dates.
