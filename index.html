∑Gap Finder
Built for the CSC Back-to-School Hackathon

You are not bad at math. One older idea is missing.

A student can understand 80% of a lesson, miss one core idea, and then watch every next lesson stack on top of that hole. Gap Finder asks a few short questions, follows the mistakes back through the topics that came before, and shows the exact idea to repair first.

How it works

  1. Pick the topic that feels hard. You answer 2 to 3 short questions on it.
  2. If you slip, the app checks the topics that topic is built on, one by one.
  3. It stops at the earliest idea you missed, explains it, and gives you a 1-week review plan.
Gap Finder covers 16 middle-school math topics, from negative numbers up to multi-step equations, inequalities and percentages. Your answers stay in your browser (localStorage) and are never sent anywhere. Built with the help of AI tools, which are disclosed in the project README.
